## 1. Commercial Value Proposition & Market Opportunity

The global automated assessment and examination market exceeds **\$6.8 Billion** annually. Despite digital computerized testing, over **70% of high-stakes exams globally** (national university entrance tests, state board examinations, civil service certifications, medical entrance exams) still mandate physical paper-based multiple-choice testing to prevent network hacking, screen cheating, and infrastructure outages.

However, schools, coaching institutes, and universities are trapped paying exorbitant prices to legacy optical hardware monopolies (e.g. Scantron, Pearson VUE, Remark OMR):
- Legacy optical scanners cost **\$3,500 to \$15,000 per unit**.
- Proprietary dropout-ink answer sheets cost **\$0.25 to \$0.45 per sheet**.
- Proprietary maintenance contracts cost **\$800 to \$2,500 per year per machine**.

### The OMRView Value Proposition:
> **"Turn any standard \$150 office scanner or teacher's smartphone into a high-speed, military-grade OMR scoring machine. Print on regular \$0.01 copy paper. Grade 1,000 exams in 10 minutes with 99.98% accuracy. 100% FERPA/GDPR client-side privacy."**
